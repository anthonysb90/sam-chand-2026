About the Event

A Room Built for Leaders Like You

This is not a general conference. It is not open to the public.

You were personally invited because the leaders of four denominations believe your best leadership is still ahead of you. Come ready to grow.

Featured Speaker
Dr. Samuel R.
Chand
Leadership Architect  ·  Change Strategist  ·  Dream Releaser

Dr. Sam Chand has spent more than three decades helping pastors, ministries, and organizations grow beyond what they thought was possible.

Raised in a pastor’s home in India, he has served as a senior pastor, college president, chancellor, and President Emeritus of Beulah Heights University — which grew to become the country’s largest predominantly African-American Christian college under his leadership.

Today, Dr. Chand personally consults, mentors, and coaches some of the largest church pastors in America. He has authored more than 41 books on leadership, been named to the top-30 Global Leadership Gurus list, and endorsed by leaders including John C. Maxwell and Pastor Craig Groeschel.

His singular life vision: Help Others Succeed.
